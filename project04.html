Project 4: Top Spinner

The design used a 1:3 gear ratio. The launcher was a 36-tooth gear, while the gear on the top spinner was a 12-tooth gear. This allowed the top spinner to rotate at a very fast speed since there was a significant torque to the launch. My idea was to minimize the width of the spinner to maximize the speed of the spinner, which would allow it to spin longer. Because the bottom gear was too close to the desk surface, the spinner occasionally hit the desk, which disrupted its motion.
